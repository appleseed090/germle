Outbreak!

Vaccinate 4 vaccines left

Vaccinate 4 people to break up the network

How to play

  1. Tap people to vaccinate them. The outbreak starts when you have exhausted all vaccines.

  2. Once the outbreak starts, you can quarantine one person per day.

  3. The game ends when the outbreak has nowhere left to go. Save as many people as you can!

Save % or more and the outbreak is Contained; below that, it Spread.

3 Healthy, 2 refuses vaccines, 4 infected. Each number counts that person's contacts still in the network.
